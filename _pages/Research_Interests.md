My research directions include:

<div class="research-directions">

  <section class="research-direction">
    <h2>Safe AI Decision-Making</h2>
    <p class="research-question">How can we make sure AI decisions in power and energy systems are safe before they are used in the real world?</p>
    <figure>
      <img src="/images/research/safe-ai-decision-making.png" alt="Safe AI Decision-Making research direction figure">
    </figure>
  </section>

  <section class="research-direction">
    <h2>Scalable Real-Time Optimization</h2>
    <p class="research-question">How can we make fast and reliable decisions for energy systems with millions of solar panels, electric vehicles, buildings, data centers, and devices?</p>
    <figure>
      <img src="/images/research/scalable-real-time-optimization.png" alt="Scalable Real-Time Optimization research direction figure">
    </figure>
  </section>

  <section class="research-direction">
    <h2>Coupled Cyber-Physical Systems</h2>
    <p class="research-question">How can AI understand and optimize interconnected systems such as power grids, buildings, transportation, communication networks, and data centers?</p>
    <figure>
      <img src="/images/research/coupled-cyber-physical-systems.png" alt="Coupled Cyber-Physical Systems research direction figure">
    </figure>
  </section>

  <section class="research-direction">
    <h2>Robust and Secure AI</h2>
    <p class="research-question">How can AI remain reliable when data is noisy, sensors fail, conditions change, or systems are under cyberattack?</p>
    <figure>
      <img src="/images/research/robust-and-secure-ai.png" alt="Robust and Secure AI research direction figure">
    </figure>
  </section>

  <section class="research-direction">
    <h2>LLMs and AI Agents for Decision Support</h2>
    <p class="research-question">How can language models and AI agents turn rules, text, logs, and expert knowledge into better decisions for complex infrastructure?</p>
    <figure>
      <img src="/images/research/llms-and-ai-agents-for-decision-support.png" alt="LLMs and AI Agents for Decision Support research direction figure">
    </figure>
  </section>

  <section class="research-direction">
    <h2>AI for Energy, Energy for AI</h2>
    <p class="research-question">How can AI help build cleaner and more efficient energy systems, while energy systems support the growing demand of AI itself?</p>
    <figure>
      <img src="/images/research/ai-for-energy-energy-for-ai.png" alt="AI for Energy, Energy for AI research direction figure">
    </figure>
  </section>

</div>
